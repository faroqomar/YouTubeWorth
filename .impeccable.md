## Design Context

### Users
The owner uses Worth while watching mostly English videos about software, AI, reviews and learning. The job is to find useful moments while preserving understanding and necessary context.

### Brand Personality
Calm, clear, practical. Confidence should come from understandable behavior and honest evidence.

### Aesthetic Direction
Compact English settings, warm off-white surfaces and restrained green accents. No marketing hero or technical controls on the main screen. Simple controls with a collapsed Advanced section, as explicitly chosen by the user.

### Design Principles
- Explain consequences in everyday language.
- Preserve context and existing playback controls.
- Use progressive disclosure; avoid extra steps.
- Keep keyboard focus, readable contrast and narrow layouts usable.
- Show implementation and evidence limitations accurately.
